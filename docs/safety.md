# Safety

Auto Retry throws replies away and asks for new ones. Most of the time, that's all it does. But there's one setting I won't release without explaining, and one way of using it that I want to be honest about. That's what this page is for.

## Who this is for

Adults.

Lumiverse itself isn't an adult app. Anyone can use it, and it has no adult content of its own. My extensions are different. I made them for adults writing their own fiction.

If you're under 18, these extensions aren't for you. If someone under 18 asks me for help with anything that may be sexual, such as their retry notes or their prompts, I won't help with it.

The extension can't tell what your scene is about, and it doesn't try to. Every check asks one question: did this reply fail as writing? It came back empty, stopped mid-sentence, broke character, or refused to go on. It only reads the text of the reply, and it doesn't keep that text once it's decided. It knows nothing about you.

## The setting that asks before it turns on

Under Refusal tuning there's **Also catch it stopping to offer support**. It's off by default. Turning it on opens a warning you have to accept. Nothing else in the panel asks you first.

It catches a reply where the scene stops and the model talks to you instead of the story. For example, it says what you wrote worries it, tells you to talk to someone, or gives you a support line. If your character is the one in trouble, the model may think it's happening to you.

Sometimes the model isn't wrong. The same message can be welcome for one person and unwanted for another. The extension can't tell which one you are, because it only sees the reply.

So it's your call: do you want that kind of reply thrown away and asked again? I want you to choose that knowing what it does, not find out later.

You can turn it off again with the same setting. Turning it off doesn't undo what happened while it was on.

## What the retry loop can turn into

Auto Retry keeps asking until a reply passes its checks. For a reply that got cut off or came back empty, that's the whole point. For a reply you didn't like because of what it said, it's something else. You're asking until you get the answer you wanted.

The twentieth reply comes from the same model as the first. It's the same request again, with a different random result. The nineteen you threw away came from the same place as the one you kept. Asking again doesn't make the answer more true.

People have been hurt after spending a long time with a model that kept agreeing with them. The setting above throws away one kind of reply that might have disagreed with you. That's why it's off by default and asks before it turns on.

## What that looks like from the inside

I'd rather be specific than guess how you feel.

One of these on its own might mean nothing. Several of them, again and again, are worth noticing:

- You retry a reply because of what it said about you, not because of how it was written.
- You raised **Most tries per message** because the retries wouldn't give you the answer you wanted.
- You turned on the support setting because a reply upset or worried you, not because it broke a scene.
- You keep replies because they agree with something really negative you believe about yourself.
- The chat has become something you spend a lot of your day waiting for, and you often feel worse after.

None of this means you're doing something wrong. It means Auto Retry might be making it easier to keep going when stepping away would help more.

## If some of that applies to you

Honestly? I'd uninstall it. Don't just switch it off for now. This extension makes asking again easier, and sometimes easier isn't what you need.

To remove it and everything it saved:

1. Press **Reset…** at the bottom of the settings panel.
2. Press **Tick every setting**, then tick **Delete saved presets** underneath.
3. Press **Reset ticked**, then **Yes, reset**.
4. Press **Save**.
5. Uninstall the extension.

If you also clear this site's storage in your browser, the last few local settings go too, like where the panel sat on screen and the list of chats you switched it off in. Your chats and characters belong to Lumiverse, and the extension doesn't send them anywhere.

I get nothing out of you keeping this installed. Removing it is a completely fine choice, and I made it easy to remove on purpose.

If you'd rather talk to a person, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists free support lines by country. It's not an emergency service, and I'm not assuming you need one. It's here because some people would rather talk to a real person.

## From me

The model writes well. That's not a complaint. But it doesn't know you, and it doesn't care what happens to you. When it writes something kind, it's producing text from the conversation and its instructions. When it writes something cruel, it's doing exactly the same thing.

A reply can sound personal without knowing anything about you.

If you kept a reply because it agreed with something awful you believe about yourself, that reply doesn't prove it. It was one result. The next one from the same model could say the opposite. A model agreeing with you doesn't make it right.

Writing something isn't the same as wanting it. Adults use fiction to explore things they'd never want in real life, gentle or harsh. Having a kink for something isn't the same as wanting it to happen. I don't have a problem with any of that.

There's one line I hold, whatever the reason: sexual content involving a minor. I don't support it, and that includes fiction. I don't think it helps anyone. I think it can make harm to a child feel less wrong, first in a story and then outside of one. I'm not accusing you of anything by saying this. It's where I stand, and it's a big part of why I made these extensions for adults.

This is the one part of the extension where I get to talk to a person instead of a settings panel, so I'll just say it: I hope you're okay. If things are hard right now, talk to someone you trust. A language model can't help with that the way a person can.
